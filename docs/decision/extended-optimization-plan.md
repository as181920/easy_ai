## Experiment sequence

1. Execute v0.5: verify token order, masks, answer spans, positions, gradients, transfer, resume and metrics; compare separate and joint encodings from the own v0.1 parent with fresh optimizers. Use the reviewed 128-row core, seed 1337 and at most 1,000 updates per arm. Require per-language balanced accuracy >=99% and both actor/event mixed-group correctness >=90% twice consecutively. Confirm a qualifying recipe with seed 2027. Follow v0.5's supervised stages only after those gates pass. Its original maximum remains 7,000 updates.
2. If fitting fails, diagnose the surviving error slices and train at most four additional 1,000-update fitting recipes. Candidate interventions are positional representation/scale, candidate-only pooling, fresh initialization versus own-parent transfer, and a staged simple-to-mixed relation curriculum. Select the concrete changes from observed evidence, record each hypothesis and config before execution, and change one factor relative to a named reference where possible. Do not repeat already failed unchanged experiments. At most one additional architectural intervention may replace an unneeded fitting recipe if inspection demonstrates a representation bottleneck; it consumes the same budget and requires deterministic correctness tests.
3. If fitting is repeatable but held-out transfer fails, run at most two 2,000-update supervised recipes addressing demonstrated composition/coverage or known/unknown forgetting. Freeze complete-family splits, qualified sources, exposure and metrics before training. Use second-seed confirmation for the selected candidate, at most 2,000 updates. Do not consume fresh acceptance for recipe selection.
4. Reserve at most 1,000 updates for a second-seed follow-up fitting confirmation, and at most 2,000 for an evidence-driven final corrective comparison. The extra ceiling is 13,000 updates; combined with v0.5, the entire goal permits at most 20,000 optimizer updates. Unused budgets are not instructions to train. Each failed recipe retains its own stop rule; budget exhaustion supports only a resource-bounded conclusion.
5. Once a recipe qualifies, freeze the selected checkpoint and profile, calibrate separately, evaluate reserved acceptance once, and measure CPU/CUDA inference and memory. Any subsequent acceptance-informed correction requires a newly reserved panel and must explicitly count prior acceptance as observed development.

External pretrained weights, teacher distillation and RL are not default rescue experiments. If evidence makes one materially necessary, document the changed scientific question and resource feasibility before choosing it within the remaining finite recipe budget. Do not silently change a from-scratch result into a pretrained-model result. Prefer reusing existing implementations over creating a parallel training framework.
