# Decision v0.6 — conditional supervised recipe

Historical phase plan, now completed. See [outcomes](v06.md) and the subsequent [85% development-policy amendment](v06-diagnostic-policy.md). Original thresholds below describe the measured trajectory; the frozen pre-training copy is preserved.

This elaborates the conditional follow-up in [the scaling plan](v06-plan.md) before any supervised follow-up trains. The controlled factorial, gates and budget remain frozen. Reviewed natural candidates and quotas are reserved without consulting model predictions. The own v0.1 preview remains delivered until every release gate passes.

Proceed only after all registered controlled screens/extensions finish and the frozen selection names a known-mastery-qualified cell. Use its data scale, controlled checkpoint and capacity. If it selects an enlarged capacity, compare its base-capacity counterpart at the same data scale, using the counterpart's registered endpoint. If it selects base, run one recipe; duplicating the same base cell adds no comparison. These follow-ups study a new curriculum, not an isolated capacity effect.

Each recipe allows at most 3,000 additional updates with a fresh AdamW optimizer, learning rate 0.0003, no warmup, no dropout, effective batch 32, and the separately measured full-shape microbatch (base four, large one). The broader data include all training-world profiles at the selected scale, newly reviewed natural records, previously qualified natural training records, and the existing bilingual MASSIVE routing replay. Check all inputs against the original 256/64 caps without truncation. Freeze all source/data/review/config hashes before training. No external weights or tokenizer retraining.

For 500 known-stage updates, each batch has 16 complete-group controlled known examples, eight label-balanced natural examples and eight routing examples. Recheck known mastery at updates 250 and 500 on the fixed training probe and canonical development. Require two consecutive checks; otherwise stop at 500 and retain the failed outcome. This guards against losing the selected controlled capability while introducing natural/routing supervision.

Only after this retention check, introduce unknown judgments for the remaining 2,500 updates. Each batch has 12 controlled known examples in four-query groups, eight controlled unknown examples in complete assertion pairs, six natural examples and six routing examples. Rotate controlled language/axis buckets, alternate languages for unknown/natural/routing, and choose natural labels before examples to compensate for the heavily skewed available Chinese source pool. Three-option natural supervision includes agreed OCNLI neutral examples; BoolQ has no natural unknown labels. Full-candidate routing remains 18-way.

Natural candidates: 200 training and 32 development examples per language before review, plus separate calibration/test reservations. The conservative inventory takes the first encountered candidate per record, then filters supported lengths; it contains 56 fresh Chinese contradiction examples. Other hypotheses for the same records are discarded, so this is a limitation of the chosen inventory, not an exhaustive source-availability claim. Training quotas are skewed; do not claim a balanced raw pool or 200 qualified examples per language. Target-blind manual review by the same Codex assistant retains only source-label agreements and excludes disagreements rather than relabelling them. This is not independent human validation. Record actual counts. Preserve the unreviewed original reservation and the hash-ordered blind exports.

Natural reservations occur during the controlled-screen work and before supervised follow-up. No natural model predictions inform reservation or review. The original reservation's broader wording about the 56-example inventory is corrected in a separate provenance note without changing its frozen samples or hashes.

New natural development is independent of the previous local materials by literal-record exclusion. Binary natural development uses equal-sized yes/no subsets per language, ordered by fixed review-ID hash, before any model prediction. Joint natural development retains all agreed labels. This prevents a skewed majority predictor from meeting the accuracy floor. Small reviewed panels still have substantial uncertainty. Previously observed v0.5 natural development is a separate regression diagnostic, never fresh acceptance. The current English natural source is BoolQ; it does not test English natural unknown judgments.

The controlled seen-combination panel guarantees training exposure for the actors in its recorded facts. This is a pure seen-identity slice for binary known queries. In joint mode its absent-actor unknown queries can include other shared-pool names not observed as facts in the 32-world training subset; do not describe those unknown-query results as a fully seen-vocabulary experiment. The separately required novel-actor panel still tests both known and unknown judgments on held actor identities.

Every 250 updates, evaluate binary and (after retained known mastery) joint development. All three required controlled slices must independently meet the existing accuracy/class-recall, actor/event-group and own-parent gain gates. Reviewed natural accuracy must be at least 65% in each language with no greater than three-point parent drop; retain the old natural diagnostic in reports. Selection prefers an eligible joint profile, otherwise an eligible binary profile; rank by worst controlled group, worst controlled balanced accuracy, then NLL. Save eligible checkpoints, not an unqualified best-looking final model. Checkpoints before retained known mastery cannot qualify for delivery.

After the permitted primary recipes reach their terminal budgets, freeze one eligible full recipe or a negative selection. An eligible recipe receives second-seed confirmation from the original own parent: repeat its complete controlled stage and then the same supervised curriculum. The confirmation must pass the same profile and all gates, within the shared 30,000-update ceiling and the predeclared 7,000-update maximum full confirmation. No eligible primary means no supervised confirmation, calibration or acceptance. Fresh calibration/test material is opened only after a confirmed eligible full recipe, with fixed checkpoints/profile. Do not silently extend these terminal budgets.
