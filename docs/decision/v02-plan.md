# Next bounded iteration: factual decision scoring

Status: **completed bounded iteration**, authorized and evaluated on 2026-10-02. Both 2,000-update arms, clean independent evaluation, calibration, runtime checks and the handover are complete. CE improves controlled factual scoring, but English binding, Chinese uncertainty and routing retention still fail; the margin does not justify promotion. See [v02.md](v02.md) for measured results and reproducible artifacts. v0.1 remains the delivered preview. The user clarified 80% is advisory. This round uses one shared model, own trained weights, Ruby/Torch.rb, GPU-first execution within 4,096 MiB, and model-only scope.

## Evidence constraining the choice

- The 6.63M architecture with our own parent fits a 128-row bilingual diagnostic completely, including binding. Capacity is not proven insufficient simply because open expressions fail.
- Earlier grouped binding examples raise average held-out accuracy 80.03% to 82.55%, but seeds regress and mixed-group correctness remains weak. Curriculum and group sampling are useful ingredients, not an untried cure.
- Broad supervised task coverage increases natural-source macro accuracy from 42.43% to about 61%, mainly on news/request domains. QA/NLI regressions, poor binding and withheld Emotion remain. Generic public NLI CE alone has already been tried; mere reuse is not a novel intervention.
- RoPE and sinusoidal broad results are approximately tied; no broad architectural superiority is established.
- Evidence supervision improves evidence selection without adequate answer improvement. Short MLM warm-up lowers MLM loss but previously hurts downstream macro accuracy. Neither is the next default.
- v0.1 independent request-domain accuracy is 78.25% English/81% Chinese. This is domain classification, not proposition truth. The observed 16-case negation diagnostic has 0/4 complete pairs in each language. These examples are regression material, not new acceptance.

## Procedure and decision points

1. **Freeze behavior and references before fitting.** Use identical diagnostic inputs for v0.1 and the own broad sinusoidal parent. Check affirmative/negative factual claims, actor swaps, negated questions, insufficient information and equivalent candidate wording. Include state/question removal or shuffling as sensitivity diagnostics, not newly labelled gold tests. Confirm distinct token sequences, valid labels, candidate-order invariance and real parameter updates. Use these observations to select the initializer before opening any fresh acceptance panel; do not add an initializer sweep.
2. **Build task-aligned supervision with genuine diversity.** Reuse eligible public gold training data, supplement with audited paired facts and natural paraphrases across at least eight event/domain families in Chinese and English. A tentative pilot cap is 20k–40k training rows, not a claim that this quantity creates general semantics. Pair labels come from explicit recorded facts or reviewed annotations, never word-presence rules. Include negatives of irrelevant facts and affirmative answers to negative questions to prevent `not => false` shortcuts. Introduce an insufficient-information option when the task lacks enough evidence; two-option softmax cannot express a missing third answer. Split entire facts, variants and translations together, and reserve novel expression/event families. Audit available unused natural held-out material before freezing counts; acquire additional public data if necessary. Downloaded row count is not actual exposure.
3. **Verify fitting on the new supervision, then compare one intervention.** Reuse the existing fitting machinery on 128–256 diverse training examples, with fixed-set CE and complete-pair correctness. Near-perfect fitting is a diagnostic direction, not a shipping gate. If it fails, inspect gradients, actual updates, masks, targets and optimization before a larger fit. For the bounded pilot, arm A uses answer CE with replay; arm B uses the exact same rows and paired batch schedule plus a supervised pair-margin term. This isolates the extra objective rather than confusing it with new data or sampling. For each verified opposite-label pair, score the affirmative-minus-negative candidate margin on both states and penalize failure to meet the correct opposite signed margins. Do not force opposite answers for uncertainty or non-complementary options. CE remains the primary objective. The margin weight is fixed before the pilot, not tuned on acceptance.
4. **Keep compute and architecture bounded.** Preserve the shared 12k tokenizer, four-layer 256-wide sinusoidal encoder and existing scorer. Proposed allocation: 50% natural factual QA/NLI, 30% audited contrast examples, 20% routing replay, matched between arms. These proportions are initial engineering choices, not proven optima. Pilot one seed, maximum 2,000 updates per arm, microbatch four/accumulation eight, eval every 100, own initializer and identical tensors/row visits, no teacher/RL/growth. Fix the LR schedule after the fitting diagnostic. Log visits/unique groups/tokens, not just corpus size. If a clear validation improvement appears, confirm with a second seed before the final test; preserve failures. If it does not, stop and diagnose instead of extending an unsuccessful trajectory indefinitely.
5. **Select for behavior, calibrate for probability quality.** Report individual accuracy and complete-pair correctness separately by language/phenomenon/domain, including worst cells and candidate-wording robustness. Freeze a validation selection rule before training: prioritize mean factual-task accuracy among checkpoints satisfying a predeclared routing-preservation tolerance, tie-break by factual NLL. Define that tolerance from the measured baseline and intended use, not retrospectively from the winning checkpoint. Log NLL/Brier and preserve full metrics even when selection emphasizes correctness. Add raw batch loss, clearly labelled 50-update moving average, a fixed training probe in eval mode, held-out loss/accuracy/pair correctness, pre-clip gradient norms and parameter-change checks to reports. A moving average is presentation only and must not conceal failure.
6. **Open independent evaluation once and deliver the measured result.** Freeze a few hundred independent contrast groups per language and a natural factual panel before the pilot, subject to the material audit. Entirely withheld event families measure transfer beyond familiar templates; existing routing/test panels are disclosed regression checks only. Temperature and review thresholds are fitted on separate calibration data after checkpoint selection. Compare arms on the same final panel, reporting uncertainty at semantic-group level. Promote a factual-scoring preview only if the measured capability is useful, improves on the unchanged baseline across languages and avoids unacceptable routing regression. Preserve v0.1 if the new candidate fails; 80% remains advisory rather than a reason to conceal weak slices.

## Conditional work after this round

If new examples cannot be fitted, prioritize implementation/optimization diagnostics. If they fit but natural held-out judgments remain poor, prioritize reviewed linguistic coverage and actual source exposure; consider a controlled foundation warm-up only after the supervised diagnosis. If adequate fitting and coverage still show a reproducible capacity limitation, compare one larger configuration within the GPU budget. Bigger size, dynamic layer growth, RoPE, joint encoding, evidence heads, MLM and RL are not simultaneous interventions in this round. A shared model handling additional languages needs real supervision and fresh evaluation in those languages, not a locale flag change.

Behavioral and contrast testing follow the evaluation principles of [CheckList](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.442/) and [Contrast Sets](https://aclanthology.org/2020.findings-emnlp.117/). These support exposing gaps hidden by aggregate accuracy; they do not guarantee that the proposed training objective will fix this model. Success of the next pilot is uncertain, and no numerical success probability is justified by the existing few-seed evidence.
