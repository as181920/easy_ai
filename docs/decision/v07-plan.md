# Decision v0.7 — training sufficiency and transferable binding

Status: authorized 2026-10-20; prospective protocol before training. Preserve v0.6 and its uncommitted implementation/results. The goal is evidence about useful learning direction on the local GPU, not a guarantee of Jev parity or from-scratch impossibility.

## Primary comparison

Use the own v0.1 parent/tokenizer, 6.63M separate/RoPE model, AdamW .0003, zero dropout/warmup, effective batch 32, microbatch 16 and exact four-query language/axis-balanced sampler. Freeze the existing v0.6 128-world training prefix and observed development views, with data/source/parent hashes. No acceptance predictions. Each of CE, entity renaming, actor-pair coupling, and their combination starts independently at seed 1337 and completes 6,000 updates. This is 192,000 visits / 8,192 rows = 23.4375 mean visits, matching the v0.6 32-world/1,500-update exposure. Compare earlier checkpoints as a learning curve; do not call matched mean exposure equal convergence or compute.

Renaming changes only person identities consistently within each four-query unit. Draw distinct replacement names from the existing shared training pool, exclude all novel-actor names, and reject rendered states matching either record order of any reserved v0.6 material. Preserve labels, negation, order and candidate meaning. This is identity recombination, not additional naturally labelled language data. Validate lengths and use zero truncation. Coverage counters count original rows, not unique augmented surfaces.

Pair coupling retains ordinary CE and adds weight .5 times mean `softplus(1 - (z_supported - z_contradicted))`, where `z = logit_yes - logit_no`. Group the two different-actor queries about the same mixed-truth record/assertion; skip homogeneous-truth and event pairs. Align by candidate IDs after permutation. CE anchors absolute judgments; the auxiliary term encourages separation across opposite-label queries. This is different from the historical independent per-row hinge and sentence-evidence head, but is still an unproven intervention.

## Conditional work and budget

All four primary recipes finish before selection. Canonical development and the fixed training probe are measured every 500 steps; all held-out views and the full original training panel are measured at 6,000 (and any 12,000 extension) endpoints. Extend CE/128 from 6,000 to 12,000 only if its final 1,000-step training-probe trend improves worst-language balanced accuracy by at least one point or worst actor/event group correctness by at least three points; otherwise record its plateau without extending. Preserve the fixed 6,000 endpoint separately.

Relative to CE/128 at the same 6,000 updates, a directional improvement requires at least five points on worst-language actor-pair correctness across canonical, seen-combination and novel-actor views, with no more than three-point balanced-accuracy regression in any required view/language. Evaluate every 500 steps; retention diagnostics use 95% training balanced, 85% actor/event groups in both languages, a 55% canonical development floor and two consecutive checks. Retention does not block the fixed training-sufficiency comparison or erase partial improvement.

The four arms form a complete renaming × pair-loss factorial, allowing an interaction to help even if either isolated intervention does not. Select by worst held-out actor group, then worst balanced accuracy, then NLL; confirm an improving selected recipe and its independent CE reference at seed 2027, 6,000 updates each. Do not require a production release gate to confirm a promising direction. If no primary/authorized combined intervention improves, execute a separately frozen pretrained-encoder diagnostic to distinguish own-foundation limitations from decision-head/training limitations. Preserve the changed pretrained question and language/resource limitations. The Ruby stack remains preferred; test compatibility and memory before committing to a pretrained architecture.

Hard ceiling: **42,500 newly executed optimizer updates**, including at most 500 probes/diagnostics; maximum 42,000 own-model updates (24k primary + 6k CE extension + 12k confirmation). A pretrained diagnostic, if needed, is frozen separately and uses at most the remaining 500 diagnostic updates. Unused budget is not a request for additional search. Record every successful update, including replay; inherited historical work is not new work.

Tests assert deterministic logic rather than training convergence. CUDA probes exercise 64/64-token shapes for every variant within a 4,096 MiB process budget before learning. Training requires actual CUDA; fallback invalidates that run for comparison.

Keep fixed and extended endpoints, raw prediction audits, exposure, clipping/gradient diagnostics, source/data hashes, checkpoints and failure records. Report observed held-out row and pair accuracy with counts and the 25% independent-guess pair baseline. Deliver charts, reproduction/loading commands, compact records and updated README/handover/retrospective. A broad factual release needs a subsequent declared scope and independent evaluation; this goal may finish with a useful confirmed directional improvement or a bounded negative conclusion.
